Plain-English guide

ChessLab is one local web app. It opens in your browser, keeps your settings and saved games in that browser, and uses Stockfish for suggestions when the engine is available.

  • Play is a live game against the engine.
  • Openings lets you build a line and practise from its last move.
  • Review walks through an analysed game one move at a time.
  • Progress summarises saved results.
  • Settings controls the account label, theme, engine strength, and data.
